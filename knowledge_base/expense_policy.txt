Sample Business Expense Policy

This sample policy is for testing the RAG knowledge base and is not an official company policy.

Approval: Get manager approval before making a business purchase over $100. Purchases over $500 also require department-head approval.

Reimbursement: Submit an expense report with an itemized receipt within 30 days of the purchase. Include the business purpose and project or client name when relevant.

Meals: Reasonable business meals may be reimbursed when they have a clear work purpose. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Travel: Book standard economy airfare and reasonably priced lodging. Obtain manager approval before booking travel.

Personal expenses: Personal purchases, traffic fines, and expenses without a business purpose are not reimbursable.

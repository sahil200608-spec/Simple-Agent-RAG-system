Sample Leave Policy

This sample policy applies to full-time employees. It is for testing the RAG knowledge base and is not an official company policy.

Annual leave: Employees receive 18 paid annual-leave days per calendar year. Submit requests to your manager at least five working days in advance when possible. Approval depends on team coverage.

Sick leave: Employees receive 8 paid sick days per year. Tell your manager as soon as practical when illness prevents you from working. A medical certificate may be requested for absences longer than three consecutive working days.

Parental leave: Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of parental leave. Contact HR at least 30 days before the expected start date when possible to discuss eligibility and arrangements.

Unused annual leave: Up to 5 unused annual-leave days may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried days must be used by March 31 or they expire.
